The menu bar widget now has the solid panel from the design instead of see-through Liquid Glass, in light and dark. In dark mode its cards are a step lighter so they stand apart from the panel. A library card now folds and unfolds from anywhere along its left edge. When the widget is too tall for the screen and folds every library, the chevrons keep working: opening one opens that library alone, so the widget no longer gets stuck folded. A link picker that does not fit stays open, with the libraries after it and the groups left to the window while it is.
